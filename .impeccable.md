# .impeccable.md

Design context for this project — used by AI agents to make consistent, informed design decisions.

## Design Context

### Users

Mixed audience: recruiters evaluating fit, potential collaborators scoping a partnership, freelance clients assessing capability, and curious visitors browsing. They arrive with varying levels of technical depth. The job to be done is **quickly forming a positive impression** — answering "is this person someone I'd want to work with?" The emotional outcome should be: _"Wow, this is a good website — and this person clearly cares about their craft."_

### Brand Personality

**Warm. Playful. Human.**

The portfolio belongs to a real person, not a product or agency. The tone is casual and carefree — confident without being corporate, expressive without being loud. It should feel like meeting someone interesting at a conference: friendly, curious, and quietly impressive. Fun is welcome; polish is expected; stiffness is not.

Voice & tone: conversational, a little personality-forward, never jargon-heavy. The site should feel like it was made by a human who enjoys their work.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Visual tone:** Soft, light, and crafted. The current blue-on-white canvas palette (`brand: #3AB0FF`, `canvas: #F7FBFF`) is the right foundation — it reads as calm and considered. Elevate it with moments of warmth and personality rather than replacing it.

**Theme:** Light mode only. No dark mode required.

**References:** Sites that feel genuinely well-made and earn a "wow" reaction — not because of flashy effects, but because of the care in details. Think personal sites from indie developers who clearly enjoy design. NOT corporate product marketing (no Stripe-ification), NOT loud agency scroll-jacking, NOT heavy glassmorphism.

**Anti-references:** Loud agency sites with oversized hero text and aggressive scroll effects. Anything that feels like a template. Cold, corporate "product page" aesthetics.

**Animations:** Subtle and intentional — Framer Motion is already in use. Prefer elegant micro-interactions over dramatic entrances. Reduced-motion must always be respected.

**Layout:** Bento grid is the established pattern. Projects are the main focus. Personal callout should stay minimal and secondary.

### Design Principles

1. **Craft over flash** — Every detail should feel considered. Prefer refined subtlety to attention-grabbing effects. The "wow" comes from quality, not spectacle.

2. **Human first** — This is a person's portfolio, not a product's landing page. Warmth, personality, and a sense of real authorship should come through in copy, layout, and interaction.

3. **Projects lead** — The work is the proof. Layout hierarchy should always route visitors toward projects. Supporting content (bio, links, stats) enriches but never competes.

4. **Mobile is not an afterthought** — All interactions must work without hover. No critical content hidden behind hover states. The bento layout must remain legible and usable on small screens.

5. **Motion with purpose** — Animations should aid comprehension or add delight, never distract. Always include `prefers-reduced-motion` overrides. No physics simulations or bouncy spring effects.

### Tech Stack

- **Framework:** React 19 + TypeScript + Vite
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS with custom design tokens (see `app/globals.css`, `tailwind.config.ts`)
- **Animation:** Framer Motion
- **Runtime/Tooling:** Bun
- **Data:** Project metadata in `data/projects.json`; GitHub stats fetched at runtime
